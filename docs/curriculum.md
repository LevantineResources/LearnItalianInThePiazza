# Curriculum

Goal: from zero to practical A2 Italian for travel. The player can get by in cafés, restaurants, shops,
stations and hotels, ask for and follow directions, meet people, and handle small problems.

### Grammar so far
- Articles with nouns (un/una, il/la), masculine/feminine agreement (questo/questa, mio/mia, amico/amica).
- Present tense, io and lui/lei: vorrei/vorrebbe, posso/può, mangio/mangia, sono/è, ho/ha.
- tu vs Lei forms: stai/sta, scusa/scusi, puoi/può, hai/ha, sai/sa, ti/Le presento.
- Negation with *non*. Questions by intonation plus question words (dove, come, quanto).

## Still to cover for travel A2 (a working list, for planning)

- Numbers past 20, times, days; *Che ore sono? A che ora …?*
- Shopping: sizes, colours, *quanto costa*, *posso provare?*, *lo prendo*
- Transport: train station, tickets (*un biglietto per …, andata e ritorno*), platforms, timetables
- Hotel: *ho una prenotazione*, a room for two nights, problems in the room
- Past tense (passato prossimo) to tell what happened; *ieri*, *stamattina*
- Likes and preferences: *mi piace / mi piacciono*
- Small problems: lost items (done in L6), at the pharmacy, *mi fa male …*, asking for help
- Plans and the near future: *domani vado …*, *vuoi venire?*
- Larger review lessons where tiles are removed (as in L6)
